## Camera

_Moves the picture itself: punches, drifts, shakes._

### `drift`

Slow cinematic push (or pull) across the whole window.

_default length 6s · aliases: `slow-push`, `ken-burns`_

| Option | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `amount` | `0.12` | Zoom gained over the window (negative pulls back). |
| `pan` | `0.02` | Sideways drift as a fraction of the width. |

### `pulse`

_default length 4s · aliases: `beat`, `bounce`_

| Option | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `bpm` | `120.0` | Beats per minute. |
| `amount` | `0.06` | How hard each beat hits. |

### `punch-in`

Quick punch-in toward the speaker, holds, then eases back out.

_default length 1.6s · follows the face · aliases: `zoom`, `lean-in`, `push`_

| Option | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `zoom` | `1.4` | How close to get (1.0 = none, 2.0 = double). |
| `target` | `'face'` | What to push toward. (face / center) |
| `hold` | `True` | Ease back out at the end. Turn off to stay zoomed. |
| `snap` | `0.18` | Seconds the push-in takes. |

### `shake`

Impact shake that starts hard and settles, like a bass drop.

_default length 0.7s · aliases: `impact`, `rumble`_

| Option | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `intensity` | `1.0` | 1.0 is a firm hit, 2.0 is an earthquake. |
